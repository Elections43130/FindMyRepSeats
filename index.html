What can you run for?

Type your home address in Fairfield County, Ohio. You'll see every elected office on your ballot, from your precinct up to President, with what each one does, when it's up, and whether it pays.

House number and street is enough. Route numbers like St Rt 158 work too.

Know your precinct instead?

Districts come from the Ohio Secretary of State voter file (September 2026). Offices come from the Fairfield County Board of Elections. Party offices shown are Democratic ones. Always confirm your districts and filing deadlines with the Board of Elections before you file.
