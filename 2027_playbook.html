2027 Local Playbook

Every city and village office in Fairfield County that comes up in 2027, with what it took to win last time and how many Democrats already vote in that odd year. Start with the blue cards: seats nobody filed for.

Sources: Fairfield County Board of Elections official canvasses (2021, 2023, 2025) and the county voter file. "Last time" is the most recent election for the same seats: 2025 for Lancaster council and president of council (2-year terms), 2023 for mayors, Lancaster citywide offices, Pickerington and village councils (4-year terms). "Win target": for one seat, half of last turnout plus one; for several seats, one more vote than the last-place winner. "Already voting our way" counts voters whose last party primary was Democratic and who voted in that odd year; unaffiliated voters are not counted, and most of these races are nonpartisan, so neighbors matter more than labels. "Sleepers": Democrats who voted in 2024 but skipped that odd year. 2023 turnout was unusually high because of the abortion and marijuana issues. Terms and seat counts should be confirmed with the BOE before filing. Fairfield County portions only; Canal Winchester, Columbus, Reynoldsburg and Buckeye Lake are mostly in other counties and are left out.
